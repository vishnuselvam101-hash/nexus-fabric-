# CRITIC A, round 3: NEXUS FABRIC film 3 (54 s, EN and JA)

Films: `flagship3/final/NEXUS-FABRIC-film3-54s-en.mp4` and `-ja.mp4`. Judged on picture, text and rhythm only; audio not judged. SCRIPT.md was read after the first full viewing.

Verdict: not excellent. The film has one original idea (a flat pattern that rolls into the part it makes), one proof moment (the checks card) and a coherent type and colour system. It fails as a launch film. The picture does not hook in two seconds, about twenty screens run through 54 s, the AMOEBA section is unreadable, and the "not released" message repeats until it becomes the film's main claim. It becomes excellent with one hero curve carried through the film, eight or nine screens instead of twenty, type sized for a phone, and the product's real verdicts shown at product size.

Method: EN contact sheets at 2 fps (6); 30 fps strips at all ten scene changes (10); 10 EN full-size stills; JA contact sheets at 1 fps (3); 4 JA full-size stills; 390 px views of two EN stills; one detail composite (hook joint; 10 s review panel in EN and JA). Contact-sheet timings are approximate (about +/-0.5 s); strips, stills and the composite are exact. Not sampled at 30 fps: the flat-to-3D roll (12.5-13.0 s) and the blob exit (19.5-20.0 s).

## Scores

A. Hook: 5/10. Frame 0.0 is a grey tee pipe with a small blue cut arc. The first text on screen is a grey caption, "Visualisation made for this film · not a product view", which tells the viewer the picture is not the product. "One wrong curve." rises from 0.33 s and is complete by about 1.5 s; the big black type is the only element that would stop a thumb. The picture then freezes: the joint is identical in eight crops from 1.6 to 3.0 s. The cut arc sits about 110 px above the target curve at 1920 wide (about 22 px on a phone). A blue bar crosses the gap at 1.0 s (EN still) and is gone by 1.6 s.

B. Clarity: 5/10. The function reads for a fabrication audience: "Dimensions in. Pattern out." (8.3-10 s), five part shapes, "It checks its own work.", "Runs fully offline." The product name is legible only as small text in the real-app header (10.5 s) and as the wordmark at 50 s, while the script's voice says it at 8-9 s, so sound-on and sound-off viewers get different information. "Pipe", "steel" and "flat pattern" never appear as text. AMOEBA is never defined before the fine print. The "not released" labels tell a buyer the product is unfinished. A silent viewer's summary: "engineering software that makes flat patterns and checks itself, not released yet."

C. Visual design: 7/10. A real system: warm-grey field, graphite type, metal renders, amber for review and green for pass, and one blue cut line that recurs (hook arc, question loop, pattern outlines, the checks smile at 25 s). Heavy grotesk headlines share one mask-rise. The flat-pattern-to-3D idea is specific to pipe fabrication and original. Losses: the 19.0 dark blob, the 33.0 graph, captions in nearly every scene, a logo "A" that is never introduced, and mixed number formats.

D. Motion: 5/10. Scene changes land on bars and counts step every half-second, so the grid holds. Word stagger (about 0.07-0.1 s) and the AMOEBA headline settle are clean. Problems: one headline move (mask rise) on about 20 headlines, hard cuts everywhere with no shared device; static holds over 1.5 s at 1.6-3.5 s, 4.5-6.25 s, 20.4-22.0 s (empty card), 32.5-34.0 s (graph) and 44.5-46.0 s; the blue loop snaps off the left edge within two frames at 8.1 s; the chips at 42.0-42.4 s cross the frame at over 3,000 px/s and never settle.

E. Product presentation: 6/10. Best: the checks card at 25.0 s (real check names, differences such as 1.59e-12, "26 of 28", REVIEW REQUIRED) and the five parts with real dimensions ("Developed width 3,141.593 mm" at 13.0 s). The two real-software clips (10.5-11.5 s and about 40.5-41.5 s) are real UI but full-window, cut off at the bottom, with about 12 px text and no action. They read as wallpaper, not proof.

F. AMOEBA, 30-42 s: 3/10. "Change one number: 150 to 158 mm, 11 obligations raised" is the one clear idea, buried under an 18-node graph of 20-26 px type (33.0 s; about 5 px on a phone). At 38.5 s the Records and Replay audit panels enter as blank rows over node labels. The script's "superseded" and "ripple along the edges" never appear. A newcomer learns that values link; not what AMOEBA is.

G. Ending and CTA: 5/10. The end card (52.0 s) is clean: the "A" mark, the wordmark, a grey tagline, a black pill. But the CTA names no destination, the pill is about 520 px wide at 1920, the last readable line is "Not validated for manufacturing." (from 52.5 s), and nothing moves for the final 1.5 s.

H. Phone readability: 3/10. At 390 px the headlines work: "Dimensions in. Pattern out." and the "150 mm" numerals read. Card labels (about 5 px), the AMOEBA graph (nothing legible), check names, real-app text, captions, fine print and the CTA label (about 7 px) do not. The proof and AMOEBA sections carry most of the product information, and neither survives a phone.

I. Japanese version: 6/10. Picture timing matches at the points checked: the 8.0 s loop has the same geometry as EN, the 10.0 s panel content matches, and the end cards match. Most line breaks are right (寸法から、/板展開へ。; 切る前に、/どう確かめる？). Defects: "・照合" starts line 2 at 26-29 s (a line-start violation); JA headlines are set heavier than the EN headlines; the JA end card credits 音声：VOICEVOX Nemo and the EN end card carries no voice credit (check both licences); the JA grid is still English at 47.0 s, so confirm the flip frame in both films. The Japanese wording has not been reviewed by a native engineer (script note); I judged type and layout only.

Overall: 5/10.

Memorable sentence (what a viewer keeps): "It checks its own work, and it isn't released yet."

## Defects, most damaging first

1. 0.0-4.0 s, hook. Static, small, no product name, and the first text disclaims the product. The joint is frozen 1.6-3.0 s. The gap never opens, against script scene 1. Why: this is the stop-or-scroll decision. Fix: start the type at 0.0 and land "One wrong curve." by 1.0 s; delete the first caption; lift the blue cut arc 60 px over 0.4 s from 0.3 s; flash a 4 px amber gap line at the joint at 0.9 s for 0.2 s; push the camera in 4% on the joint from 0 to 4 s; name the product in a 28 px lockup from 2.0 s.

2. Whole film. About 20 distinct screens in 54 s, averaging under 3 s each, and no hero. Why: the viewer cannot hold a sentence, so B and D both fall. Fix: keep the script's order and tighten each part to one idea: hook 0-4; question 4-8; roll 8-20; checks 20-26; DXF 26-28 (two lines, then the NOT RELEASED state at product size); AMOEBA 28-36 (item 4); honesty 36-42; language and offline 42-47 as one block; end card and CTA 47-54.

3. 29.0 s NOT RELEASED stamp; 44.5-46.0 s REVIEW REQUIRED as a hero block; "Development build — not validated" at top right in nearly every frame from 30 to 46 s. The script asks for these states where they naturally appear, not as emphasis. Why: the most repeated message in the film is that the product is not released. Fix: delete the 29.0 stamp (the DXF file name at the bottom already reads NOT-RELEASED_REVIEW_REQUIRED, at product size); show state chips at product size inside the product screens; delete the top-right caption over 30-46 s; in "What it can't confirm, it shows." lead with PASS 5 at 120 px and set the inconclusive count second.

4. 28-36 s, AMOEBA. Eighteen nodes at 20-26 px, blank rows entering at 38.5 s, no definition, no "superseded". Fix (8 s slot): 0-1 s "AMOEBA" with "Records every value and step. Links them." at 88 px; 1-3 s three nodes (header_radius, s_end, delta) at 56 px joined by one blue edge; 3-5 s "150 → 158 mm" with one edge pulse and "11 obligations raised"; 5-7 s "Replay: match." with "31 records, 0 failing"; 7-8 s hold. Delete the legend, the other fifteen nodes, and any panel whose rows have no text yet.

5. 18.0-20.0 s, header with opening (19.0 still). A dark slate blob about 700 px wide and twice the tube's height, over the flat pattern. It reads as a stain and is the least-finished image in the film, at the climax of the shape gallery. Fix: render the header in the tee's metal gradient; cut a 220 x 160 px elliptical opening at 35% along the tube; trace its edge in the product's blue outline (the real screen draws branch_opening as an outline, 10.5 s); no fill; turn the part 1.0 s with ease-in-out.

6. Type size across the film. Anything under about 20 px at 1920 is gone at 390 px: card labels, check names, graph, real-app text, captions, CTA label, fine print. Fix: 40 px minimum for anything the viewer must read; 28 px for labels; the checks list at three rows of 36 px; CTA pill 800 x 150 px with a 56 px label; delete the per-frame captions.

7. 10.5-11.5 s and about 40.5-41.5 s, real-software clips. Full window, cut off at the bottom, no action. Fix: a 2x push-in on the Gates panel for 2 s with PASS and INCONCLUSIVE chips at 28 px; a cursor clicks "Record correction…" and the value changes 150 to 158 in the real field (crop and move only, per the script rule); keep the banner "Development build — not validated" in frame.

8. 4.5-8.1 s, question. Static 4.5-6.25 s. The blue loop (6.5-8.1 s) is not the joint curve, draws after the words have left, and snaps off the left edge. Fix: draw the hook's intersection curve under "how do you know?" between 4.5 and 5.5 s with ease-in-out; hold 0.5 s; exit with a one-beat lift.

9. 20.4-22.0 s, empty checks card; 42.0-42.4 s, chips. The card sits blank for about 1.5 s; the chips cross the frame and never settle. Fix: fill the check rows on the beat from 20.5 s (four per half-second); place chips in three fixed columns, each sliding 60 px into its column with a 0.15 s ease-out, one chip per eighth note, no overlaps.

10. About 10 s, number formats. The review panel reads "1.000 mm" in both films (JA still at full size; EN crop agrees), while the 13.0 s card reads "1,000.000 mm" and the cards read "1200 mm". A Japanese reader parses "1.000" as one millimetre. Fix: one number format from the product output everywhere; the panel must print the same value in the same format as the card.

11. 9.0 s, value entry. "1200" overflows its card by about 30 px, "mm" drops to a second line, and the second card sits 25 px left of the first. Fix: animate each card as one object; right-align values in a fixed box; start the count only after the card settles.

12. 8.3-10 s, headline. On screen "Dimensions in. Pattern out."; the script's voice says "Dimensions in. Flat pattern out." For a silent viewer, "pattern" alone is ambiguous. Fix: on-screen "Dimensions in. Flat pattern out." (the lead can change the locked line).

13. End card. A 1.5 s static hold, a CTA with no destination, an "A" mark that is never introduced (it appears to be the icon in the real-app header at 10.5 s), and a JA-only voice credit. Fix: lift the pill 20 px at 52.5 s; put the destination (URL or QR) in the 28 px line; introduce the mark or drop it; settle the voice credit for both versions.

14. About 30 frames carry captions ("Visualisation made for this film · not a product view", "Interface simplified…", "Actual software · development build"). Fix: one end line at 28 px; keep the dev-build banner only where the product itself shows it.

15. Japanese version. "・照合" starts line 2 at 26-29 s; JA headline weight does not match EN; the JA grid is English at 47.0 s while the EN grid flips by about 47 s (sheet, approximate). Fix: keep 出力・再読込・照合 on one line or nowrap after 再読込; match JA headline weight to EN; put the flip on the same beat in both films.
