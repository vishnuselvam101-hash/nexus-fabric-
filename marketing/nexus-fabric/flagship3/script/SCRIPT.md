## Fixed rules
- End card also carries, once and small: "Engineering platform powered by AMOEBA." (Japanese: 「AMOEBAを核とする設計基盤」) and the disclaimer "Product concept. Capabilities in development. Not validated for manufacturing." (Japanese as in ../flagship2/NEW_STRINGS.md). Owner decision, 20 Nov 2026: the product contains no automated drawing reading and none is planned unless it becomes necessary, so the film makes no claim of automated intelligence and does not announce automated drawing reading, even as planned.
- Never said or shown: approved, certified, validated, verified for manufacturing, zero errors, secure, hack-proof, customers, prices, speed or accuracy figures, standards, any statement about how other companies or tools work, and any claim of automated intelligence (the product contains none).
- Every number, name, verdict, curve, graph node and DXF line on screen is read from the product's real output at build time (engine 0.4.0). Nothing is typed in by hand. If the product shows 5 interface checks and 13 gates, the film shows those; if it shows something else, the film shows that.
- Product screens are never redrawn or retouched, only cropped and moved. REVIEW REQUIRED, NOT RELEASED and the development-build notice are never hidden where they would naturally be in frame.
- The Japanese wording has not been reviewed by a native-speaking engineer.
