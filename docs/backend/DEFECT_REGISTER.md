# Backend Defect Register

Audit of 19 November 2026. Evidence and request/response transcripts are in [BACKEND_AUDIT.md](BACKEND_AUDIT.md) (section in brackets). File references are to `crates/` at `8f352ef`.

**Severity rule used:**

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical | Wrong engineering result or wrong release state, data corruption or loss, auth bypass, or a process crash, reachable by a normal request **or** breaking the product's stated safety invariant ("APPROVED is unreachable") |
| High | Same classes of harm, but needing a fault (crash, DB error, missing file) or an operator or engineer slip; or a wrong engineering status shown to users |
| Medium | Robustness, performance, operability or security weaknesses without a direct wrong result |
| Low | Consistency, hygiene, documentation |

**Counts:** Critical 2 · High 9 · Medium 14 · Low 9 · total 34.
