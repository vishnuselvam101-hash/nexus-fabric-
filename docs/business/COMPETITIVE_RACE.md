## 8. Positioning and the factory demo

**One-line positioning**

> NEXUS FABRIC is the flat-pattern program that **shows its proof and refuses when it cannot prove** — 内寸/外寸/中立 declared where the family supports it, every convention written into the file, every quantity independently checked, every file marked NOT RELEASED until a qualified engineer signs. Development build; not yet validated for manufacturing.

Japanese shorthand for the shop floor: 「展開図に根拠を付ける。証明できなければ、出さない。」

**What we must not say in the demo**

- Not "validated", "certified" (of the product), "approved", "manufacturing-ready", "secure", "hack-proof". The word *certified* belongs to `eps_rep` only, with its scope stated.
- Not "error bound proven everywhere" — `eps_num` is allow-listed and sampling density (1.6) is not started.
- Not "works with Amada / Mitsubishi / Nazca" — no CAM acceptance has been tested.
- Not "Windows verified" — wine is not Windows; the release workflow has never run (4.3).
- Not "reproducible on Windows" — 4.1 is proven on a Linux host only, and nothing is signed.
- Not "thickness handled" — the basis is declared (1.4); the conversion is not modelled. Cutwise, Mahiro and 安田BTP are ahead of us here.
- Not "PDF input in the download" — the converter is in the product (3.1) but not in the downloadable packages (P0-2).
- Not "1398 cases on this build" — that FULL run predates engine 0.4.4.
- No competitor UI art, command names, manual text or code is reproduced anywhere in our material.
