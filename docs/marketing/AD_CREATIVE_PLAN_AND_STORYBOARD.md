# NEXUS FABRIC — Bilingual Advertisement: Creative Plan and Storyboard

Version 0.1 · 18 November 2026 · **Workflow step 1 of 7: awaiting approval. Nothing has been rendered.**

## 1. What will be delivered

Two coordinated 30-second films, 16:9, identical picture edit, language-specific voice-over, subtitles and end card: **English** and **Japanese**.

## 2. Production capability — stated honestly

Checked in the current working environment:

| Need | Available here? | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Video encoding and assembly (ffmpeg) | Yes | Editing, subtitles, 4K encode are possible |
| Photorealistic 3D rendering (Blender or similar renderer, steel materials, lighting, fabrication environment) | **No** | The photorealistic look requested cannot be produced here |
| Natural voice-over (human or neural speech) | **No** | No narration can be recorded here |
| Original music and sound design | **No** | None can be composed or generated here |
| 2D vector motion graphics rendered frame by frame (browser engine → ffmpeg) | Yes | A clean technical-illustration animatic is possible, using real pattern geometry computed by the NEXUS FABRIC engine |

Therefore two honest routes:

- **Route A — full production as briefed.** Requires a 3D/motion studio (or a workstation with a renderer), a voice artist per language (or a licensed speech service), and a composer or licensed music. This document is the brief for them.
- **Route B — draft animatic produced here.** Silent 2D motion-graphics version of the same storyboard with subtitles and end card, at up to 4K. Useful for timing, script and approval; **not** the final photorealistic film.

No render will be simulated or described as complete when it is not.

## 3. Accuracy boundary for every claim

The film is a **concept for the intended platform**. Status on 18 November 2026:

| Shown or said | Actual status | How the film handles it |
|---|---|---|
| Reads complex drawings (PDF, scans, CAD) | Planned — not implemented | Framed as what the product "is being built to" do |
| AMOEBA whole-design analysis following dependencies | Architecture decided; core records and gates implemented; investigation and dependency propagation not implemented | "Being built to"; no claim of current capability |
| 2D drawing → 3D geometry | Planned | Concept visual |
| Flat development of cones, eccentric reducers, branch cuts | **Implemented** for four families (development build, unvalidated) | May be shown using real computed patterns |
| Independent verification of results | Implemented in first form | "Independently checked" is accurate for implemented families |
| Plate division, seams | Planned | Concept visual |
| DXF generation | Implemented (basic); no CAM system tested | No claim of CAM compatibility |
| Engineer approval | Design principle; workflow not implemented | "Released only by your engineer" states the principle |

Mandatory on-screen line throughout the final 5 seconds and in the description: **"Product concept. Capabilities in development."** / **「製品コンセプト映像です。機能は開発中です。」**

Not permitted anywhere: zero-error or guaranteed-accuracy claims; "certified"; autonomous approval; factory results; customer names or logos; testimonials; statistics.

Before publication: legal/marketing review of claims, and a name/trademark check for "NEXUS FABRIC" and "AMOEBA" (noted in the research documents; not done).

## 7. Music and sound

Original, sparse: sustained low strings or synth pad with a slow metallic pulse; one rising figure at scene 3; resolve on the wordmark. No stock clichés (no drum hits on cuts, no "epic" risers). Rights: fully owned or properly licensed; documented.

## 8. Quality checklist for final review (step 6)

Subtitle text matches narration · spelling in both languages · Japanese line breaks at natural phrase boundaries · audio/picture sync within one frame at each scene change · identical picture edit in both versions · no feature claim beyond section 3 · disclaimer legible at 1080p and on a phone · wordmark consistent · no real customer data, logos or drawings in any frame · loudness consistent between versions · 4K master and 1080p deliverable both checked.
